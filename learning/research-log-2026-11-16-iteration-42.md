# Iteration 42 — preserving *future line clears*, not simply shape fit counts

Date: November 16, 2026 UTC, research branch `feature/v2.2-10x-research-gate` only.

## New bounded, evidence-driven hypothesis

The previous distributional model (iteration 41, [run 43805816034](https://github.com/welcome23bask/BlockBlastAI/actions/runs/43805816034)) improved mean survival/estimated score for all three generators across independent holdouts, particularly heavy (1.2856x/1.4863x survival), but **failed** hard safety/10x gates: normal lower-tail survival collapsed to 0.7321x on holdout 1965, and the 1966 shifted-heavy combo break rate regressed to 2.772 versus 2.757 per 100 moves. Just making space for all 28 shapes does not guarantee a future *clearing* opportunity. The previous distributional feature is kept for isolated A/B but now default OFF. The other previously rejected minimum-hard coverage, fixed three-piece future rollout, risky baseline shield, bottom-three/dual finalist pool, and proactive override also remain OFF by default.

New research-only code commit `208a343bc8c2cf6798c509a62bc3868d7979abe9` uses `BL_RESEARCH_FUTURE_CLEAR_ACCESS=1`, a deterministic, exactly legal, late-afterstate feature: for each of ten representative possible one-piece future shapes, find at most three currently legal placements which would **complete any row/column** from that candidate afterstate, using the precise 8x8 simultaneous line-clear rule. Reward distinct shape types with feasible future single-piece clears; no future tray, probabilities, next-piece RNG, hidden state, or proprietary score data is used. Assess only the top four already fully legal current-three-piece finalists per objective, preventing unbounded extra tree search; unchanged v1.7 reference compiled separately. New selftest commit `a47b95a659a2f8f712e6d3525747e71bf9d87302` requires no single-piece immediate clears on empty or fully occupied boards and a positive exactly legal five-bar row completion when top-row cells 5-7 are occupied. The saved checkpoint and four learnable weights are not modified.
