# Research direction after iterations 36–42: learned continuation value, not another static bonus

Date: November 16, 2026 UTC. **Research branch only**. This is a hypothesis/design note, **not** a training result, performance claim, production change or promotion.

## Evidence to explain

Across independent recent synthetic holdouts, local geometric features often increase normal mean survival and estimated score versus the unchanged v1.7 control, yet repeatedly regress normal p10 or shifted-heavy combo safety, while shifted-heavy survival remains ~1.02–1.28x. Run 43806245696 (iteration 42) showed 2.0630x and 2.2664x normal mean survival but only 1.2319x/1.2032x shifted-heavy mean survival, with shifted-heavy p10 falling to 0.9412x on one holdout. Even excellent *immediate* clearing ability does not estimate the probability of surviving more than one unseen tray. No 10x; official game scoring has **not** been measured. The strict gates remain.

## Public prior art to evaluate, not copy wholesale

* https://github.com/rshk941/block-blast-solver : exact current-3-piece search plus a learned *state-value* continuation network; board occupancy, per-row/column counts, holes and future piece placeability as inputs; temporal-difference replay with target network and 8-way rotation/flip augmentation. The author explicitly reports limitations and does **not** establish a 10x result on our generators.
* https://github.com/zacharygoh/block-blast-solver : exact three-piece lookahead, transposition reuse, distribution-robust future danger assessment and empirical distribution testing. It explicitly warns truly adversarial piece generation can force death, and labels game-mechanic assumptions unverified.
* https://github.com/Botkraker/block-blast-AI : legal-action masking and survival-based training rewards rather than only simulated score. Its environment and shape distribution are independent approximations, not the proprietary live Block Blast algorithm.

## Distinct bounded follow-up hypothesis

Implement a **training-only survival continuation target** (e.g., winsorized logarithm of remaining moves plus a strong lower-tail failure penalty), collected from complete **synthetic** episodes using *training seeds only*, across separate normal/heavy/shifted-heavy generators; never leak holdout seeds or hidden future RNG into current decisions. Prefer a tiny regularized value estimator with observable board-only features rather than a large external ML dependency. **Do not apply naive 8-fold D4 replay augmentation**: the current 28-shape synthetic catalog is not closed under rotations/reflections, and per-generator shape probabilities are not invariant. See `learning/audit_shape_symmetry.py` and the exact catalog prior audit; any legal distribution-preserving transform must be proved before adding it. The policy should still enumerate exactly legal visible three-piece routes and use trained continuation predictions **only** to rerank a bounded, diverse list of verified legal current-tray afterstates. Condition the feature on remaining empty-space topology and known combo deadline, rather than fixed bonuses on every move. Make an explicit distributional safety objective that ranks candidates on **worst generator** and low-decile survival. Keep a no-change ablation and the unchanged separately compiled v1.7 control.

Before training, build a cheap diagnostics/selftest pass proving feature encoding preserves legal piece masks, row/column clears and combo accounting. Test any *proposed* geometric augmentation against the exact 28-shape catalog and the generator's shape probabilities; when not distribution-preserving, skip it. An independent catalog symmetry audit found only the identity transform is closed over all 28 current shapes (reflection/rotation variants omit 2–13 of the 28 source shapes), so blindly following a public project's 8-way replay recipe would introduce invalid training examples here. Bounded one-epoch train on new audited cross-role epochs, stage-2 safety selection on disjoint games from its training epoch, then paired 30-game/generator holdouts on **two entirely fresh epochs**; preserve failure artifacts. Measure mean and p10 survival, synthetic estimated score, illegal moves, combo breaks per move, avoidable deadlines, 4096 cap and search truncation. Maintain all strict >=10x gates with independent replication and no censored games. All value-target and reward quantities refer to the offline simulator, not the real game.

**Stop conditions / safety:** do not run concurrently with existing Actions research; do not reuse reserved train or holdout epochs; never overwrite validated checkpoint, deploy, touch main, build the iPhone dylib or edit live v3.4. Do not promote a model that merely improves average survival while regressing p10, combo breaks or any generator. Avoid spending excess CI minutes: if measured time indicates a single epoch cannot finish under existing 12-minute cap, reduce **training samples**, not gate thresholds or independent holdout size. A genuine >10x claim requires both strict heldouts and independent replication, not a TD training loss reduction.
