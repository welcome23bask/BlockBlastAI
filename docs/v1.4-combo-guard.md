# v1.4 Combo Guard / Streak Tracker — experimental

**Motivation:** Real user feedback: PINK COMBO achieved a displayed **53,000** score but often lost its clearing streak in crowded, difficult boards. Whether a 10x game score multiplier was used for this run has not been established.

## What the code changes

1. **Verified combo tracker survives short scan interruptions.** `BLComboTracker` records the last independently verified 8x8 board and three tray shapes, the legal clearing streak, and a capture timestamp. When a screen read fails, the gameplay hint is cleared for safety, but the streak anchor is preserved. On the next confirmed frame, it keeps the streak only if the board/tray match or a **single unambiguous legal move** is detected within 1.75 seconds. A gap over 4 seconds, incompatible move sequence, manual pause/edit, or recorder restart resets confidence.
2. **No more permanent global penalty for breaking a streak.** Initial v1.4's 100,000-point beam penalty did save last-chance clear opportunities, but the 80-game hidden-seed benchmark lost 13.5–14.8% average moves on two mixes. It was rejected.
3. **Conservative last-chance combo rescue.** In the final legal nonclearing move of a *verified* active streak, the solver considers first moves that clear a line, but only if they leave a full legal solution for all remaining visible pieces. The alternative must keep immediate occupied-cell count no higher, first and final board-quality evaluations no lower, and future-shape access no lower than the baseline. Otherwise it keeps the prior v1.3 planner's original choice. **No guarantee of preserving every combo**, since no safe clearing move may exist.
4. **Status panel now reports verified streak and how many placements remain before a clearing move is due**. The panel cannot claim a combo when it hasn't witnessed a valid clear.
5. The **pink/wood/blue/multi-theme scanner, v1.0-like BLINK guide capture behavior, resize/move UI and on-board overlay** are unchanged.

## Tests and actual outcomes

- Regression tests passed for a difficult 36-cell board with a due-next-move combo, an impossible-to-keep combo, recovered capture continuity, false/ambiguous move rejection, real reconstructed Bazz1TV clears, board vision, full-tray solver and simulator.
- Full iPhone ARM64 build succeeded (GitHub workflow `build-v14-combo-guard.yml`, run **43608840697**).
- Early global-pressure algorithm was rejected: 100k combo-break penalty made average survival worse by **13.53% and 14.76%** in two 80-game uniform tests despite reducing deadline skips.
- Less aggressive targeted rescue with loose gates also worsened those uniform tests by **3.16% and 9.87%** on average.
- **Final STRICT safety-gated version** vs frozen v1.3 PINK COMBO, each 80 seeded simulated games:
  - 1st uniform: 228.68 vs 228.68 average moves, 8 vs 8 skipped immediate clear opportunities.
  - Difficult-piece stress: 42.35 vs 42.35 average moves, 4 vs 4 skipped immediate clears.
  - 2nd uniform: 221.81 vs 221.81 average moves, 11 vs 11 skipped immediate clears.
  - No illegal placements. **This means the new last-chance override was never selected in those 240 cases.** We cannot claim that the refined decision engine plays better or keeps more combos under those scenarios. Its confirmed benefit is **retaining trustworthy combo history across brief scan errors**.
- Tests use approximate unverified piece distributions and a **score proxy**, not the real mobile game's exact points or hidden upcoming trays. They cannot establish 1M/100M readiness.

## Field test protocol

1. Back up the working PINK COMBO and PINK BLINK SAFE `.dylib`.
2. Install only one in your signed Block Blast IPA. Check the same pink theme to confirm all 8x8 blocks and three tray pieces are recognized.
3. At a streak break, open the panel and note whether it says `Streak N, clear within M move(s)` or `Combo not yet verified`.
4. If a legal single-line clear exists while streak is due, compare the AI's suggested placement with the clearing placement. Include a full screenshot **before touching any block** and a screen recording of a few moves if possible.
5. Compare unmodified scores, multiplier settings, actual turns survived and breaks; don't compare a 10x run against a normal run.
6. Roll back immediately if it reads the board incorrectly or recommends an illegal placement.

**The new version is experimental and unverified during live iPhone gameplay.** Strict preservation constraints mean a crowded board may still lose its combo when no safe complete-tray alternative is available.
