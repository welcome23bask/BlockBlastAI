# BlockBlastAI v1.2 — Steady Highlights + Multi-Theme Recognition

**Experimental branch:** `feature/v1.2-steady-multitheme`. This is based on the working source of v1.1 and does not replace the prior v1.0/v1.1 builds.

## Problem addressed

The previous overlay hid the entire yellow placement outline during every ReplayKit scan and also removed it during the first unconfirmed observation of a changed board. At approximately four scans per second, that causes visible flashing. Separately, the adaptive image classifier still required certain saturated RGB colors and missed muted, pale, or unfamiliar block skins.

## Changes

- **Steady highlight:** outlined board tiles stay rendered during capture. The AI still hides the movable bubble briefly to prevent overlay text covering a tray shape, but never deliberately hides the placement borders for ordinary scanning.
- **Hold until confirmed:** a single nonmatching frame does not remove a suggestion. Only a confirmed board/tray change replaces the plan; confirmed expected placements can be processed on the first fresh verified frame. Short scanner anomalies preserve the last verified outline for up to four seconds. Prolonged errors, app interruptions, or an untrusted board clear suggestions for safety.
- **Theme-independent recognition:** fallback classifier compares tiles to per-frame board/tray backgrounds, including per-slot tray background colors and noise-sensitive thresholds that recognize muted tile colors without interpreting the board's empty interior as occupied.
- **Prior performance preserved:** scanning cadence remains 0.24 seconds, with async 192-state beam planner, unchanged iOS C ABI and fast next-move recognition.
- **No new permissions** or private game-memory hooks. No automatic dragging or block placement.

## Research and coverage

Official publisher's page promotes **new styles**: https://www.blockblast.com/ .
Official iOS app user reviews and community posts report changing palettes and skins (pink, coffee, watermelon, metallic, glass, etc.). Example: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485 and https://www.reddit.com/r/blockblast/comments/1sh1hja/is_this_rare_at_all/ .

There is no verified, exhaustive list of every current or future skin. The algorithm is designed to adapt to colors rather than claiming to have hard-coded or confirmed every variant.

## Automated validation

- Existing legacy-blue vision, wood-board recognition, orientation inversion, and no-fake-board checks.
- New seven-palette synthetic fixtures: wood, pink-on-pink, neon purple, coffee/cream, watermelon colors, monochrome-metal, pastel mint.
- Each fixture tested with and without a yellow, persistent highlight on an empty board cell, both upright and upside-down ReplayKit rows.
- Existing piece-placement solver, randomized legality, next-move transitions, and simulator tests.
- GitHub macOS ARM64 dylib compilation.

The synthetic fixtures are not screenshots of all released skins. Real device tests are still necessary for newer themes, textured tiles, changing gradients, line-clear animations and capture performance.

## On-device checklist

1. Back up working v1.1 IPA. Inject only v1.2 dylib into a compatible IPA, signing with KSign.
2. On a fresh board, confirm three tray shapes and exact 8x8 board alignment.
3. Observe yellow outline while doing nothing for at least 20 seconds; it should remain steady through ongoing scans.
4. Place the recommended piece and verify that the outline changes only when the updated board/tray is recognized.
5. Change or trigger different theme colors and verify each piece shape and occupied grid cell.
6. Open panel and trigger capture errors; no stale guidance should persist indefinitely.
7. If any tile color or patterned skin fails, capture a screenshot of that appearance. Never trust an unverified highlight.

**Important:** A GitHub build and synthetic tests do not prove live iPhone functionality. Zero-delay highlighting and universal skin recognition cannot be guaranteed.
