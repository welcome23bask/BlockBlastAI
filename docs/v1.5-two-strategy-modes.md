# v1.5 — Two strategy modes, inspired by public solver behavior (not a site integration)

## Status and provenance

The user compared BlockBlastAI's highlighted placement with **blockblastsolverss.com** using screenshots at a displayed game score of 37,824. The website offered two choices: `Best · clears 5 lines` and `Keep combo · clears 2 lines`. The public site describes legal move sequence comparison and screenshot analysis, but no documented reusable solver API or license for the actual engine was found in its associated public repository (which only contained a README and a redirect page at time of inspection).

**This project does not copy, scrape or connect to that website's private engine.** Instead it independently implements an 8×8 three-piece search with two explicit objective rankings, locally within the injected iPhone library. No network call or screenshot transfer to the site occurs.

## Verified exact input from supplied screenshot

0-based rows 0-4 have filled columns 3 and 4, and rows 5-6 have a filled column 6. This totals 12 occupied cells. The three tray slots show a 3×3 square, a four-cell L `.#/.#/##` and a second 3×3 square. The prior high-score and combo streak cannot be inferred from this screenshot.

The exhaustive solver checks **12,337 search nodes** for that exact game state:

| Choice | Cleared lines | First clear | Complete tray |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| BEST: MAX LINES | 5 | 3rd piece | Yes |
| KEEP COMBO | 2 | 1st piece | Yes |

One verifiable MAX LINES solution, in 1-based board coordinates:
1. Left 3×3 square: rows **6–8**, columns **1–3**.
2. Middle L: rows **6–8** at columns **7–8**; vertical arm in column 8 and bottom-left arm in column 7.
3. Right 3×3 square: rows **6–8**, columns **4–6**.
After the final placement, rows 6/7/8 and columns 4/5 all clear in one placement. This yields **5 total simultaneous lines** without overlapping any occupied cells.

The KEEP COMBO objective prioritizes a valid clear earlier in the placement sequence, then total lines, then useful remaining space. On the screenshot, a two-line route has a clear on the *first* move and can be preferable **if** the current streak will expire before move 3. The site's exact private sequence is not reproduced or claimed.

## Two-mode behavior

- **MAX LINES:** prioritize placing the entire visible tray, then most lines cleared across all three placements, then future board openness. This can break an expiring combo when its large clear occurs on the third placement.
- **KEEP COMBO:** prioritize placing the entire visible tray, then preserving an *observed* active combo or creating a new clear, then earlier line clears, total lines, and remaining board shape capacity. The observed streak is still provisional because real game scoring details aren't publicly verified.
- The search compares legal **piece orders and positions** while accounting for simultaneous row and column clears after every placement. A 180,000-node budget prevents unbounded UI latency. If the search is cut short, the app explicitly labels the result **budget-limited**, not proven globally optimal.
- The app shows a two-option `UISegmentedControl` in its movable/resizable scrolling panel. Changing modes discards the previous suggestion and requires fresh board confirmation to avoid showing the old mode's move.
- Auto-scan, pink/blue/wood/multi-theme vision, v1.0-style blink highlights, legal transition checks, and persistent confirmed combo tracking remain. The two-mode solver does NOT place blocks automatically, modify the game's score, or predict the hidden next pieces.

## Regression tests

- Existing blue/wood/pink/seven-palette vision, solver and combo tracker tests must continue to pass.

- A passing CI build and synthetic simulation do **not** guarantee the game's real scoring, combo timing, image detection, or long-run survival. Preserve prior installed v1.4 and v1.1 BLINK builds for rollback.
