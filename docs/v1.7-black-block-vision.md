# v1.7 BLACK BLOCK Vision: dark-blue board and orange brick tray

**User-reported failure:** Green/blue skins and varied color palettes frequently caused v1.6 SMART SURVIVAL to show `AI …` without any placement guide. User chose to standardize on the solid-black occupied block skin. Supplied screenshot: 709×1536 portrait, bright-blue game background, navy 8×8 grid, nearly-black filled cells, orange brick tray pieces.

## Verified screenshot observation (source private, not committed)

On the exact supplied JPEG, legacy `BLVisionAnalyzeRGBA` returned failure. The dedicated black-on-navy implementation now returns:
- Board mask `0x808000000001e100` — **7** black occupied cells: R2 C1,C6,C7,C8; R3 C1; R7 C8; R8 C8.
- Tray piece 1: **1×5** horizontal; piece 2: **1×4** horizontal; piece 3: **1×5** horizontal.
- Board geometry: first cell center ≈(78.4,397.4), last center ≈(630.6,949.6) at 709×1536.
- Also passed when screenshot was resized to 430×932 and 393×852, matching point-sized iPhone frames. Both image orientation and exact expected layout are covered by the synthetic regression test.

## Technical change

The new `black_theme_scan` in `src/BLVisionCore.c` runs **before** the old bright-block palette detector, which was unable to recognize black square centers:
- Finds the dark navy board by the already-tested structural boundary locator.
- Samples **grid seams** to establish a blue/navy background even if many cells are occupied.
- Requires a strongly blue-toned board and at least one genuinely almost-black occupied cell. It compares 3×3 central samples, not shadows and borders.
- Independently recognizes orange/brick-red tray cell colors against the blue background, reconstructing 1×4 and 1×5 bars accurately, including when one/two slots are empty.
- Rejects contradictory geometry and impossible piece counts. If the strict black skin condition is not present, falls through to the original navy-color and green/pink/wood adaptive scanners.
- The existing SMART SURVIVAL, KEEP COMBO and MAX LINES modes, blinking **yellow placement guides**, continuously visible floating AI button and optional untrained YOLO/Core ML hook are unchanged.
