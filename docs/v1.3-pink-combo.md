# BlockBlastAI v1.3 Pink + Combo — capture fix and verified streak tracking

**Source:** User's screenshot (score displayed **480,530**) showing pink-theme board, no accepted AI detection and three clearly visible pieces. User also supplied a 4x Bazz1TV gameplay recording, analyzed separately in `docs/bazz1tv-video-field-study.md`.

## Confirmed root cause and replay

- Before fix, exact screenshot at 832x1792 resized RGBA is rejected by v1.1/early-v1.3: `BLVisionAnalyzeRGBA(...)=0`.
- Board geometry is located, but the pastel 3x3 tray piece has center RGB about (249,122,172) against tray background about (234,148,172), only **41 units** total RGB difference. The fixed palette and its 89-unit cutoff reject it.
- With per-slot local RGB background and low-noise adaptive threshold, **the supplied image itself** is decoded as **15 occupied cells** and these three pieces:
  - Left: 4-cell L `#./#./##`
  - Middle: 9-cell `###/###/###`
  - Right: 6-cell rectangle `##/##/##`
- Verified at both 832x1792 and 430x932 normalized sizes. Note: local screenshot replay is not the same as a live ReplayKit run.
- Old navy/blue and wood skin regressions remain mandatory.
- Added a synthetic fixture representing the actual screenshot's geometry and pastel colors to CI, plus seven other skin variants. The user's PNG itself is not added to the public repository.

## Verified across-tray combo tracking

- Adds `BLComboContext` and `BLSolveWithCombo` while preserving `BLSolve` ABI.
- A combo clear can occur **within three placements** (up to two nonclear placements). After three verified nonclears, the streak expires. This represents the Bazz1TV rule of thumb, **not independently confirmed internal game scoring**.
- Counts only legal **observed** transitions between confirmed board and tray states; handles the case in which the final tray piece immediately triggers a three-piece refill.
- Unknown or contradictory transitions reset combo confidence rather than fabricating a streak. Score numbers and AI recommendations alone do not advance the counter.
- Initial score and pre-existing combo prior to injection are **unknown**; tracking begins at the first verified clearing event.
- Shape-accessibility and future-clear potential remain survival priorities; combo gains are bounded and reduced when the board is dangerously crowded.
- The existing v1.1-style guide layer flash during capture and movable UI are preserved.

## Two binaries, distinct reliability claims

**PINK BLINK SAFE (recommended once iPhone ARM64 CI is green):**
- Pink/other theme-relative scanner and original v1.1-style blinking guide layer.
- Compiled with `-DBL_SAFE_BASELINE_SOLVER=1` and the frozen `benchmark/v11_blink_solver.c`.
- This is the **known user-tested move planner** with a new vision implementation; real iPhone pink-theme detection must still be confirmed.

**PINK + COMBO EXPERIMENTAL (research only):**
- Includes the new 3-move combo logic, 256-state survival beam, future-shape clearance.
- Linux holdout 40 uniform seeds: average +0.9% moves over v1.1, **P10 worsened from 71 to 40**; 40 large-piece stress seeds: average +14.1%, **P10 worsened from 20 to 18**. Both strict survival gates FAIL; uncertainty intervals include no improvement.
- Therefore it is not justified to label the experiment superior in survival or 100M-ready.

## Test plan and release limitations

1. C regression `tests/test_pink_theme.c`: detect the pink board and three shapes, reject a blank menu, reject wrong orientation, match 15 occupied bits.
2. `tests/test_palette_variants.c`: wood, pink-on-pink, neon, coffee, watermelon, metal, mint, including mirrored screen and a simulated yellow guide outline.
3. `tests/test_combo_tracking.c`: two nonclear moves preserve streak, third nonclear breaks streak, actual row clear verified, refill verified, reject phantom moves.
4. Original solver legality, full-tray rescue and simulator tests.
5. ARM64 iPhone .dylib compilation, then independent long-run comparison against frozen v1.1 BLINK.
6. Actual **iPhone** tests: confirm AI changes from “AI …” to a valid piece hint on the supplied pink skin; verify the highlight follows exact board coordinates for at least 20 successive placements; check combo tracking when screen is temporarily obscured or ReplayKit restarts; preserve the stable old IPA for rollback.

**This build has not been demonstrated to reach 100 million points.** Exact Bazz1TV scoring behavior, hidden tray generation, device latency and future pieces remain unverified. Compile and synthetic tests cannot replace on-device validation.
